A place to dump the things I keep meaning to write. Some of these will become
real posts; some will quietly wither. That's the point of a garden.

## The summer

The plan for the next few months is mostly one big thread: rewriting
[open-ttp](/projects#open-ttp) in Go, running the trajectory sims through an adapted
isentropic-flow model, and then comes the ambitious part - building the whole thing
into an actual avionics bay. Hardware. Solder. Things that can catch fire.

I want to document this properly rather than as a single triumphant write-up at
the end, so expect a dev-log series in the style of the NSSC day-one / day-two
posts.

## Things I want to write

- **Why I'm rewriting open-ttp in Go** - the kickoff post. What's wrong with the
  current version, why Go, and what "good" looks like by September.
- **Adapting an isentropic-flow repo for trajectory sims** - the physics, and
  the unglamorous reality of bending someone else's code to a purpose it was
  never meant for.
- **From sim to silicon** - turning a simulation into an avionics bay. This is
  the one I'm most nervous about and therefore most want to write.
- **A follow-up to [On IRC](/blog/on-irc)** - less history, more "here's how I
  actually use it day to day in 2026."
- **How the activity feed on this site works** - the AT Protocol plumbing behind
  the little panel on the home page.
